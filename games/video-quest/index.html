Articles: a / an / the

We use a or an when we talk about one thing in general.

a

a washing machine

an

an air conditioner

the

the house

💡 Use an before a vowel sound.

Hi, my name is Sarah and I will say 10 things about myself using articles "a" and "the" and I'll talk about what is in my house.

Okay. I have a washing machine. I also have a fridge. I have a small stove. I want a bigger stove.

I don't have an electric toaster. I do have a vacuum cleaner, and I have a TV.

I have an air conditioner, and I have a microwave. I want another TV.

So how about you? Can you say 10 things using articles about things in your house?
